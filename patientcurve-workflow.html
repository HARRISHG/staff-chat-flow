What your clinic gets

More reviews. More patients in the chair.

More Google reviews

Every happy patient is asked, at the right moment.

  • No visit is missed. Every completed visit gets a rating request, not just the patients your team remembers to ask.
  • Asked while the visit is fresh, 3 hours afterwards, with one reminder if they forget.
  • Happy patients go straight to your Google page. 4 and 5 stars get your review link in one tap.
  • Unhappy patients reach you first. Low ratings stay private and your team is alerted to call, before it becomes a public review.
More patient flow

More enquiries booked, and more patients coming back.

  • Every enquiry gets an instant answer, day or night, and can book straight from the chat.
  • Nobody who enquires is forgotten. Three follow-ups go out until they book.
  • Missed appointments are rebooked instead of lost, by message and on your call list.
  • Past patients come back. Anyone with no visit for 6 months gets a warm invitation to return, and visited patients hear from you every month.
Fewer empty chairs

Patients turn up, or free the slot in time.

  • Two reminders before every appointment.
  • Rescheduling takes a few taps in the chat, so patients move a visit instead of skipping it.
  • Cancellations alert your front desk straight away, so the slot can go to someone else.
A calmer front desk

The follow-up runs itself. Your team makes the calls that matter.

  • One call list, most urgent first, instead of notes and memory.
  • Routine messages go out automatically, so busy days don't stop follow-up.
  • A daily summary shows the owner exactly what happened.

Your front desk

The team's day, in one staff chat

  • Today's visitsEvery appointment today. Mark each one done or didn't come.
  • Call listWho to call, most urgent first: call-back requests, missed visits, then new enquiries. Log each call in a tap, and they come back on the list when it's time to try again.
  • Book for a patientFor phone calls and the front desk. Staff can also add a patient to a time that's already taken.
  • Add a walk-inThey get the same follow-ups as anyone who enquired.
  • Find a patientBy name or part of the phone number, with their next visit.
  • Instant alertsCancellations, call-back requests, patients who want to chat, and low ratings.
  • Daily summary at 6 pmThe last 24 hours (enquiries, bookings, cancellations, visits, no-shows, reviews, help requests), plus tomorrow's appointments.
Built to be polite

Rules every message follows

  • No messages at nightNothing goes out between 9 pm and 8:30 am. Anything due then waits until morning.
  • Out-of-date messages are cancelledIf a patient books, reschedules, cancels or opts out, the messages that no longer apply are dropped.
  • Patients stay in controlThey can stop messages from the chat at any time and turn them back on whenever they like.
  • Sensible booking limits30-minute slots within your opening hours, at least 1 hour's notice, up to 14 days ahead. A patient can hold one upcoming visit at a time; your team can book more.
  • Private feedback stays privateOnly happy patients are pointed to Google. Unhappy ones reach your team first.
