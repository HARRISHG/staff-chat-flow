Before final pricing

Ten questions to answer together

Your answers turn the example figures above into your real numbers and fix the scope.

  1. Which PMS does the clinic use?Name and version.
  2. Can the PMS connect to other systems?An API, an export, or neither.
  3. What are the consultation fees?Offline and video, and any free first consultation.
  4. What is the average treatment value per converted patient?Across the treatments the ads promote.
  5. What share of enquiries book an appointment today?Even a rough figure helps.
  6. How many enquiries a month are lost today?Enquiries that never book.
  7. How does follow-up work today?Who calls, when, and how outcomes are recorded.
  8. What are the booking rules?Days, hours, slot length, notice period, and separate times for video.
  9. Does the patient need to pay before booking?For example, for video consultations, and how they would pay.
  10. Exactly what should the WhatsApp flow collect?Every question to ask the patient, and every answer the clinic approves.
